Groups at the door

Every time three or more people are in the picture at once, the office computer counts how many of them it named and keeps one picture of the moment. With Whole people tracking a person whose face was never found counts as missed; with Faces only such a person is not counted at all. Someone walking away from the camera counts as not named, so read the rate on the morning's arrivals.
